Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers. Here's everything you need to know about our engineering process, pricing models, IP ownership, and team ramp up.

We provide two transparent engagement models: Fixed Price Milestone Contracts for clearly scoped projects with guaranteed budgets, and Dedicated Engineering Pods (Staff Augmentation) where you hire pre vetted senior full stack developers on a predictable monthly retainer with zero placement fees or markup penalties.
You own 100% of all intellectual property, source code, and design systems from Day 1. All pull requests, architecture docs, and CI/CD pipelines are pushed straight to your private GitHub or GitLab repositories under strict mutual non disclosure agreements with zero vendor lock in.
We operate synchronized shifts guaranteeing a minimum of 4 to 5 hours of real time overlap with North American (EST/PST) and European (GMT/CET) business hours. Our technical leads join daily standups, review PRs live on Slack/Teams, and provide video async updates to eliminate communication delays.
MCP is an open standard enabling frontier AI models (such as Claude and Gemini) to securely communicate with internal databases, private APIs, and custom enterprise tools. We design sandboxed MCP servers with RBAC authentication so autonomous AI agents can safely query real time company knowledge without data leakage.
Most dedicated engineering pods begin shipping pull requests within 3 to 5 business days. We perform an initial code architecture audit, replicate your Docker development environments, and align on coding conventions so there is zero friction entering your sprint cycles.
