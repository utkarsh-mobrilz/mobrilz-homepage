About Atyuttama

AI, app and web development company in Hyderabad

We design and build mobile apps, high-performance websites, custom enterprise software and production AI for startups and established businesses in India and worldwide.

What we do

Atyuttama is an AI and custom software development company based in Hyderabad, India, working with startups and enterprises worldwide. We bring product design, mobile app development, full-stack web engineering, and production artificial intelligence together in one team, so you don't have to stitch a product together from separate vendors.

  • AI solutions — copilots, calling agents, and automation
  • Web development — fast, SEO-friendly sites and web apps
  • App development — iOS and Android apps
  • UI/UX design — product design and design systems
  • Digital marketing — SEO, paid, and content
Plan AI around a complete workflow

Explore AI copilots, AI calling agents, and workflow automation with clear requirements for data access, integration, evaluation and human review. Agree permissions, hosting, ownership and operational support as part of the project scope.

Industry context and outcome-focused solutions

Explore our work across healthcare, fintech, SaaS, ecommerce, real estate, and education. If you are starting from a business problem, our solution guides cover AI agents, voice AI, MVP delivery, software modernization, process automation, and hiring a senior AI development team.

Why teams choose us

A local Hyderabad product team with global delivery; design, code, and AI under one roof. Define milestones, review points, acceptance criteria and post-launch support around the work your project needs. See our work, read client testimonials, or learn our story.
